# Joe Papadatos
### Commercial strategy, revenue growth and practical AI

I help businesses connect customer insight, sales, marketing and leadership so they can make better decisions and grow.

My work combines more than 30 years of commercial experience with an increasing focus on AI-assisted research, structured evaluation and workflow development. I’m interested in the gap between knowing what needs to change and helping people act on it.

I built and led Icon Visual Marketing, a multidisciplinary agency of more than 40 people, through to its sale in 2023. Today, through Two Icons, I work with founders and leadership teams on revenue growth, sales and marketing alignment, leadership capability and practical implementation.

## What I bring
- **Commercial judgement:** Assessing customer value, business priorities, profitability and the practical consequences of a recommendation.
- **Sales and marketing:** Key accounts, positioning, customer insight, go-to-market planning and integrated delivery.
- **Leadership and capability:** Helping people clarify responsibilities, share knowledge and reduce dependence on the founder.
- **AI output evaluation:** Reviewing business advice for evidence, reasoning, missing context and practical usefulness.
- **AI-assisted development:** Translating business problems into requirements, prompts and prototype workflows, then reviewing and refining the results.

## Current work: Sentio

Sentio is an early-stage prototype shaped by my experience working with founders.

As businesses grow, important knowledge often remains with the founder: customer history, judgement, standards, relationships and the reasons behind decisions. I’m exploring how AI can help bring that context together and support clearer thinking, stronger teams and more informed decisions.

My role centres on defining the business problem, shaping the questions and workflows, and testing whether responses make sense in realistic business situations.

The work includes reviewing responses for missing context, unsupported assumptions and practical decision value. Sentio remains in development; its working repositories are private.

[Read the Sentio project summary](https://github.com/joecpapadatos-coder/joecpapadatos-coder/blob/main/SENTIO.md)

## How I work with AI

I use ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude for research and analysis, and Cursor and Codex to support prototype development.

I bring the commercial context and judgement, use AI tools to develop and test ideas, and review the output against the original problem. The aim is to improve human capability and decision-making.

My work is AI-assisted. Project summaries will distinguish my business, design and evaluation contribution from software implementation supported by AI tools.
