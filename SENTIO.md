# Sentio
## Founder knowledge, team capability and AI-assisted decision support

**Status: early-stage prototype in development.**

Sentio is an exploration of how AI can help founders make business knowledge more useful to their teams. It is shaped by my experience building a business and working with founders.

## The problem

As a business grows, important context often remains with the founder: customer history, standards, relationships, judgement and the reasons behind decisions.

Access to information alone does not necessarily lead to better decisions. People also need to understand the context, ask useful questions and recognise what is missing.

Sentio explores how AI-assisted questions and workflows might help close that gap.

## My contribution

My role centres on:
- Defining the business problem and the needs of founders and their teams.
- Translating those needs into requirements, questions and prototype workflows.
- Reviewing responses for missing context and unsupported assumptions.
- Assessing whether outputs are useful for practical business decisions.
- Refining the approach using my commercial and leadership experience.

I use AI tools to support research, analysis and software implementation. This is AI-assisted development, and this summary distinguishes my business, design and evaluation contribution from that implementation support.

## How I review outputs

The questions guiding my review include:
- Does the response address the business problem?
- Does it use the available context rather than make unsupported assumptions?
- What information is missing before a recommendation can be trusted?
- Does the reasoning make sense in a realistic business situation?
- Does it help a person make a more informed decision?

## Current limits

Sentio remains a prototype. This summary does not claim validated client outcomes or production readiness. Its working repositories remain private. No private code, client information or internal source material is included here.
